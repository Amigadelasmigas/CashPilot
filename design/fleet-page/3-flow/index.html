  • A copy is a second container of a service that already runs on the box. It uses the same account.
  • Its traffic leaves through the proxy. The provider sees a second device on another IP, and pays it too.
  • The original does not change. It keeps your own IP.
  • If the proxy goes down, the copy stops. It never uses your own IP.
  • Some providers forbid proxies. CashPilot asks you to accept that risk first.
