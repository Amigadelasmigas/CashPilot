What each level means

Urgent: money is lost or work stopped. Overdue reminders, a disabled account, a worker offline, a rejected credential.

Warning: act within 7 days, or something stopped. A deadline in 7 days or fewer, "Did the payout arrive?", a stopped earner, a proxy down.

Info: everything else. A balance ready to cash out, a collection note, a new version.
