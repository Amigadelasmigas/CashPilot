Minimum level for pushes

What is urgent?
Urgent
Money lost within 3 days or overdue, account disabled, credential rejected, box offline, Docker unreachable, an earner sent mail or reached the LAN.
Warning
A reminder inside 7 days, "Arrived?", earner stopped or stalled, payout question, reading failed for a while.
Info
Everything else: a balance ready to cash out, notices, forecasts, spikes, updates.
