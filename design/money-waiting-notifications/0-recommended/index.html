What each kind includes
Money waiting reminders
A reminder reached a new warning stage: a claim, a payout, a balance to keep active, a login to renew. More than 3 at once arrive as one summary. They show on the Dashboard, never in the bell, as today. obligation
Payout questions
A balance dropped: was it a payout? payout
Collection problems
A collector failed: login rejected, provider unreachable, or the provider's page changed. A note on a reading, or a device not on the account. A saved login expires soon: this one shows on the bell here, but its push is a Money waiting reminder, so the Money waiting Push box controls it. The provider disabled the account. collector notice credential account_disabled
Earning slowed
A balance stopped growing. A running copy carries almost no traffic. flatline stall
Earners and workers
An earner stopped, or is missing from a box. A proxied copy or its tunnel is down, or a proxy reached its traffic cap. A worker cannot reach Docker, or went offline. An automatic app update was undone, or the old app did not start again. container missing proxied proxy_cap docker worker image_update
Activity alerts
An earner sent mail, or reached into your home network. An unusual hour of traffic shows in the bell only and never pushes. activity_mail activity_lan activity_spike

The names in grey are the kind a webhook receives.

Which problems are urgent

Urgent means money or earning is at risk now: a provider rejected a login or disabled an account, an earner stopped or is missing, a worker is offline or cannot reach Docker, a proxy tunnel is down, an app did not start again after an update, or a reminder is overdue. Everything else waits.
